Rustcraft

Rustcraft needs Minecraft's own textures, sounds and font. They stay in your browser (they are never uploaded) and are not part of this page.

Minecraft's files (required). Pick the assets/vanilla folder from scripts/fetch-vanilla-assets.sh, or a .zip of it (or a Minecraft client .jar).

Resource packs (optional). The Barebones and Realistic styles need their packs.
