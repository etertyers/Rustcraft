- 2026-10-16 — Grayscale textures get a tint: grass top, tall grass, leaves and vines use the plains colors (grass #91BD59, foliage #77AB2F) until biomes exist (M3); lily pads use a fixed #208030. The pack's `grass_side` and water textures are pre-colored, so they aren't tinted. — Matches 1.0.0's colormap behavior for plains.
- 2026-10-16 — Tint and face shading (top 1.0, bottom 0.5, N/S 0.8, E/W 0.6) are applied in sRGB space, like Minecraft, by linearizing the vertex color in the shader. — Multiplying in linear space would make shaded faces too dark.
- 2026-10-16 — Blocks with a "front" (furnace, dispenser, pumpkin, bed foot end) put it on the south face until placement orientation exists. M4's dev hotbar has no oriented blocks, so orientation moves to M7, when the inventory can hold them. — Placeholder orientation.
- 2026-10-16 — Animated textures (water, lava, fire, portal, compass, clock) use their first frame for now. — Animation isn't in any milestone yet; revisit at M8 (water) or M11 (polish).
- 2026-10-16 — The free-fly camera moves once per rendered frame, not on the 20 TPS fixed timestep. — The fixed-timestep loop arrives with player physics (M4); a per-frame dev camera is smoother.
- 2026-10-16 — Blocks are stored as a 16-bit `BlockState`: 12-bit ID + 4-bit data value, like 1.0.0's ID + metadata nibble. Variants (wool colors, log/leaf/sapling types, slab materials, crop stages, door and bed halves, stone brick types) are per-data-value overrides in `blocks.ron`. — Birch trees (M3) and wool colors need data values; deciding the storage now keeps the save format (M6) stable.
- 2026-10-16 — Hardness, light levels, collision and render layers in `blocks.ron` are the best-known 1.0.0 values (from the 1.0.0 `Block` class). Spruce/birch leaves use 1.0.0's fixed colors (#619961, #80A755); pumpkin/melon stems use a fixed green (#30C010) until growth tinting exists. — CLAUDE.md §1 faithfulness rule.
- 2026-10-16 — **Pending block shapes** (drawn as full-cube placeholders that don't hide their neighbors): Liquid, Slab, Stairs, Fence, FenceGate, Pane, Door, Trapdoor, Rail, Ladder, Lever, Button, PressurePlate, Repeater, RedstoneWire, SnowLayer, Vine, LilyPad, Cactus, Bed, Cake, Chest, Sign, Farmland, PistonHead, Fire, BrewingStand, Cauldron, EnchantingTable, EndPortal, EndPortalFrame, DragonEgg. Built so far: Cube, Cross (flowers, saplings, grass, reeds, web) and Crop (wheat, stems, nether wart); Torch since M5. — M2 allows placeholders as long as they're listed here.
- 2026-10-16 — Huge mushroom caps (data 1–9) show the cap skin on every face until the per-face rules are built. — Approximation; huge mushrooms are ⏸.
- 2026-10-16 — Translucent blocks (water, ice, portal) are drawn with cutout transparency until the blended pass in M8. — M8 owns the transparent render pass.
- 2026-10-16 — Faces touching an unloaded chunk or the bottom of the world are not drawn. Leaves draw their inner faces (1.0.0 "fancy" graphics); glass, ice, water, lava and portals hide faces between two of the same block. — Matches 1.0.0 fancy rendering; the streaming chunk manager (M3) remeshes borders when neighbors load.
- 2026-10-16 — The `world/gen/` module from CLAUDE.md §4 is `world/worldgen/`. — `gen` is a reserved keyword in Rust 2024; CLAUDE.md is updated.
- 2026-10-16 — Chunk vertices are in world-space `f32`. — Precise to well past any distance a player reaches in a session; switch to chunk-relative positions if far-out jitter shows up.
- 2026-10-16 — `cargo run --release -- --gallery` opens the block gallery (every block and variant in rows of 16 IDs along +X, variants along +Z); plain `cargo run` opens the flat test world (9×9 chunks). — M2 acceptance.
- 2026-10-16 — Grass and foliage colors come from the pack's own `colormap/grass.png` and `foliage.png`, sampled at each biome's 1.0.0 temperature/rainfall (plains 0.8/0.4, forest 0.7/0.8, desert 2.0/0.0, ocean 0.5/0.5) exactly as 1.0.0 does. This replaces the fixed plains colors from M1. Colors are per column, not blended across borders (1.0.0 didn't blend them either). — The pack's colormaps are stylized (plains grass is #70A51C, not vanilla #91BD59); using them keeps tints consistent with the pack's pre-colored grass sides.
- 2026-10-16 — Terrain is not 1.0.0's exact algorithm (its 3D density noise isn't practical to copy): a continent noise decides ocean vs land, temperature/humidity noise pick desert/forest/plains, and height is sea level + a continental shelf + per-biome base and hill amplitude mixed by smooth weights around the climate thresholds, so borders never step (steepest neighbor difference measured: 2 blocks). Climate scales (continent 500, temperature 300, humidity 260 blocks) were tuned so 39/40 random 1,300-block flights (2 minutes at fly speed) cross all four biomes; coverage is about 28% ocean, 27% plains, 27% forest, 18% desert. — M3 "done when".
- 2026-10-16 — Surface: grass over 3–5 dirt; deserts sand over 3–5 sand and 3 sandstone; beaches of sand within a block of sea level; ocean floors sand/gravel/dirt patches; still water (9) up to Y 63; bedrock at Y 0 plus a ragged layer to Y 4 like 1.0.0. — 1.0.0 surface rules, simplified.
- 2026-10-16 — Caves are "spaghetti" tunnels where two 3D Perlin fields are both near zero, not 1.0.0's carver worms. Air carved at Y ≤ 10 becomes lava, like 1.0.0. Caves stay 3 blocks below any water so oceans don't leak. — Simple, pure per-block function; looks close to 1.0.0's winding tunnels.
- 2026-10-16 — Ores follow 1.0.0's `WorldGenMinable` vein shape and `BiomeDecorator` table: dirt 20×32 and gravel 10×32 anywhere, coal 20×16 (Y<128), iron 20×8 (Y<64), gold 2×8 (Y<32), redstone 8×7 (Y<16), diamond 1×7 (Y<16), lapis 1×6 (triangular around Y 16). — CLAUDE.md M3.
- 2026-10-16 — Trees: 1.0.0's small oak (trunk 4–6) and birch (trunk 5–7) with 1.0.0's leaf shape; forests get 10 attempts per chunk with 1 in 5 birch; plains, deserts and oceans have none (as in 1.0.0). Big oaks, spruce, mushrooms, pumpkins, lakes, dungeons, springs and clay are not generated. — Out of M3's list; can be added later.
- 2026-10-16 — Plants: tall grass (dense on plains, sparse in forests), dandelion/rose patches, dead bushes and cactus (1–3 tall, nothing beside it) in deserts, sugar cane (2–4 tall) on shores next to water. Amounts approximate 1.0.0's decorator. — CLAUDE.md M3.
- 2026-10-16 — Generation is chunk-local and pure: each chunk replays the ore veins and trees of its 8 neighbors (with per-chunk SplitMix64 RNGs) and keeps the blocks inside itself, so trees and veins cross borders seamlessly and the same seed gives the same world in any load order. Plants stay inside their own chunk. — No separate population pass; any worker can generate any chunk at any time.
- 2026-10-16 — Command line: `--seed <n>` (random from the clock if omitted), `--flat`, `--gallery`. The seed is shown in F3 and logged. — 1.0.0 picks a random seed when none is given.
- 2026-10-16 — Chunk streaming: chunks are generated one ring beyond the render distance (9) so border chunks can be meshed against their neighbors; a chunk is meshed only once all 8 neighbors exist, so it never needs a second pass. Loading is a square (like 1.0.0), nearest chunks first; chunks unload 2 rings past the render distance. Worker threads = cores − 1; at most 2 jobs per worker are queued so new nearby chunks jump ahead. — CLAUDE.md M3 chunk manager.
- 2026-10-16 — Greedy meshing is not needed yet: a 20-second flight at fly speed with render distance 9 (`cargo test --release -- --ignored flight`) never had a chunk within 4 of the camera missing, and the main thread's worst `update` was 0.29 ms (7 workers on the M1 Mac mini). — M3 says to upgrade only if vertex counts or FPS demand it; FPS still needs Kenzo's check.
- 2026-10-17 — Player movement follows 1.0.0's `EntityLiving.moveEntityWithHeading` and `Entity.moveEntity` line by line: ground acceleration 0.1 × 0.16277136 / friction³ (0.02 in the air, ×1.3 sprinting), friction 0.6 × 0.91 on the ground and 0.91 in the air, input × 0.98, gravity then drag after the move, collision Y then X then Z, 0.5 step-up. Measured in unit tests: walk 4.317 blocks/s, sprint 5.612, sneak 1.295 (§4 says ≈1.31, the wiki's rounded value), jump apex 1.2522 blocks over 12 ticks. Diagonal walking is 4.405 blocks/s, about 2% faster than straight, because 1.0.0 normalizes the input after the 0.98 scale; kept on purpose. Constants are in `config.rs`. — CLAUDE.md §4 and the faithfulness rule.
- 2026-10-17 — The player hitbox is the stored physics state and the feet position is derived from it, as in 1.0.0. — Rebuilding the box from the feet every tick let rounding overlap it with a wall by ~1e-16, and an overlapping block no longer blocks movement.
- 2026-10-17 — Sprinting starts by double-tapping W within 7 ticks while on the ground (1.0.0), or by holding Left Control (a later-version convenience). It stops when forward is released, while sneaking or on hitting a wall. Sneaking lowers the camera 0.08 and backs moves off edges in 0.05 steps while nothing is within 1 block below, like 1.0.0. — Faithful default plus one convenience key.
- 2026-10-17 — Every solid block collides as a full cube, including shapes still drawn as cube placeholders (slabs, stairs, fences, doors, cactus, beds...), and non-solid blocks don't collide. Real collision boxes (half slabs, 1.5-tall fences) come with each shape's model. — Collision matches what's drawn.
- 2026-10-17 — The selection outline is the full block cube for every block (1.0.0 uses smaller boxes for plants, torches and other thin blocks). — Pending with the block shapes.
- 2026-10-17 — Unloaded chunks count as solid walls, and the player doesn't move until every chunk within a block of the hitbox is loaded. — The player can never fall through or walk into the unloaded void, e.g. at spawn.
- 2026-10-17 — Water and lava don't collide or slow the player yet (swimming is M8); lava does no damage until M9. Falling below Y −64 returns the player to spawn until health and death exist (M9). — Later milestones own those systems.
- 2026-10-17 — Breaking is instant (mining time is M7); bedrock and other negative-hardness blocks can't be broken. Holding a button repeats every 5 ticks for breaking (1.0.0's `blockHitDelay`) and 4 for placing (`rightClickDelayTimer`). — CLAUDE.md M4.
- 2026-10-17 — Mouse presses are queued until the next tick handles them, so a click shorter than one 50 ms tick still breaks or places. — 1.0.0 reads clicks from an event queue too; reading only the held state dropped quick clicks and trackpad taps (Kenzo's "placing is laggy").
- 2026-10-17 — If macOS stops pinning the locked cursor and the hidden cursor drifts out of the middle of the window, it's moved back to the center and locked again. — Kenzo saw the cursor leave the window after half a turn. Not verified by me yet (no screen capture).
- 2026-10-17 — New `blocks.ron` fields: `replaceable` (air, fluids, fire, tall grass, dead bush, snow layer, vine) and `needs_ground` (plants, crops, cactus, sugar cane, rails, pressure plates, redstone wire, repeaters, snow layers, standing signs). Placing overwrites a replaceable block, and clicking one places into its cell. Breaking a block also removes the stack of `needs_ground` blocks on top. Wall-attached blocks (torches, ladders, wall signs) and two-block doors don't pop off yet; that needs block updates. — Data-driven per CLAUDE.md §4, closest to 1.0.0 without block updates.
- 2026-10-17 — Until the inventory (M7), keys 1–9 and the scroll wheel select from a fixed dev hotbar: stone, cobblestone, dirt, planks, oak log, glass, bricks, stone bricks, white wool (replaced by the torch in M5). The selected name shows at the bottom of the screen. Scroll down moves right, as in Minecraft; trackpads step one slot per 30 px. — M4 needs a "selected block"; the real hotbar is M7.
- 2026-10-17 — The crosshair is a white plus with a dark outline drawn by egui, not 1.0.0's inverted-color `gui/icons.png` crosshair. — Good enough until the M7 HUD.
- 2026-10-17 — Chunks the player edits stay in memory when they unload and come back when reloaded, instead of being regenerated. — Otherwise walking 11 chunks away would undo every edit; region files replace this in M6.
- 2026-10-17 — Block edits remesh the touched chunk and any neighbor across a border synchronously on the main thread (measured worst case about 1 ms per generated-terrain chunk, so about 3 ms for an edit on a chunk corner). Each chunk has an edit revision, and worker mesh results from before an edit are discarded and requeued. — The change shows the same frame, with no flicker from stale meshes.
- 2026-10-17 — Free flight is a debug-build-only dev tool toggled with F4, which 1.0.0 doesn't use. Release builds have no flying. — CLAUDE.md M4.
- 2026-10-17 — `--gallery` now starts standing on the gallery floor at (22.5, 64, −4), facing south along the first row. — There's no flying in release builds.
- 2026-10-17 — **Lighting is recomputed per chunk, not updated incrementally.** CLAUDE.md M5 asks for BFS flood fill "including removal". Every mesh job instead floods sky and block light from scratch over the chunk's 3×3 neighborhood, using the same BFS rules (each step loses max(1, opacity)). Light reaches at most 14 blocks and a chunk is 16 wide, so the result equals what incremental add/remove BFS would store. Removal and chunk borders are correct by construction. Cost: about 1 ms per chunk in release; the M3 flight test still shows no stalls (worst main-thread update 0.54 ms, no missing nearby chunks). — Far simpler than keeping stored light consistent across chunk loads and edits, with the same results. Switch to stored incremental light if a later milestone needs it.
- 2026-10-17 — Sky light follows 1.0.0's `generateSkylightMap`: 15 straight down until a block with opacity, minus each block's opacity, then spread sideways and down by BFS. Opacity is 1.0.0's `lightOpacity` with 255 as 15: leaves and cobweb 1, water and ice 3, lava, slabs, stairs and farmland 15, other opaque cubes 15, everything else 0. It's the `opacity` field in `blocks.ron`. Emission uses the existing `light` levels. — 1.0.0 values. Opaque slabs and stairs reproduce 1.0.0's dark spots under them.
- 2026-10-17 — Block edits remesh and relight their own chunks (and border neighbors) at once. Every other drawn chunk within 14 blocks is relit by a background job a frame or two later. — Keeps edits instant without paying for up to nine relights on the main thread.
- 2026-10-17 — Smooth lighting and ambient occlusion follow 1.0.0's `renderStandardBlockWithAmbientOcclusion`:
  - **Light:** each cube-face corner averages the (sky, block) light of the four blocks on the face's outer side. A block with light 0 (solid) takes the face neighbor's value, and if both side blocks are opaque, a side stands in for the hidden diagonal.
  - **Occlusion:** each opaque block among the four counts 0.2 instead of 1.0, so an inner corner is 0.4.
  - **Diagonal split:** quads split along their brighter diagonal. 1.0.0 doesn't do this; it avoids dark streaks.
  - **Plants and torches** use their own cell's light without occlusion.
  — 1.0.0 default (smooth lighting on).
- 2026-10-17 — Light becomes color with 1.0.0's overworld `lightBrightnessTable` and `EntityRenderer.updateLightmap`, in the block shader:
  - Sky light is white; block light is warm, using 1.0.0's green and blue curves.
  - Torch flicker is fixed at 1.5.
  - The sum is scaled ×0.96 + 0.03 and clamped, so nothing is fully black.
  - It uses the "Moody" brightness setting.
  - The sun is at full daylight until the day/night cycle (M8).
  — 1.0.0 formula.
- 2026-10-17 — The block shader multiplies the texture by tint, shade and lightmap in exact sRGB values (converting the texel back to sRGB and the result to linear), replacing the `pow(color, 2.2)` approximation from M1. — The approximation crushed light level 0 to pure black (Kenzo's cave screenshot was all 0,0,0); 1.0.0 gives about 2–4/255 there.
- 2026-10-17 — Torches and redstone torches (`placement: Torch` in `blocks.ron`):
  - **Attaching:** they attach to the clicked face with 1.0.0's data values: 1–4 walls (attached west, east, north, south), 5 floor. They need an opaque cube to attach to and can't go on ceilings.
  - **Model:** they draw with 1.0.0's `renderTorchAtAngle` model: a 2×2-pixel stick from four planes plus a cap. Wall torches are raised 0.2, shifted 0.1 and lean 0.4 at the base.
  - **Breaking:** breaking a block removes everything it supports: torches by their data, and `needs_ground` blocks above. This replaces the M4 "stack above" rule.
  - **Hotbar:** the torch takes slot 9 of the dev hotbar (was white wool).
  — M5 "torches light caves" has to be checkable in game.
- 2026-10-17 — F3 shows the light level where the player stands, as max (sky, block). — Makes "caves are dark" checkable.
- 2026-10-17 — The spawn search (rings of columns 8 apart, as before) also generates each candidate's chunk and needs the surface block intact with two air blocks above it. — Seed 42 spawned in a cave that had carved the surface away; a tree or tall grass on the column could also swallow the player.
- 2026-10-17 — **Saved worlds** live in `saves/<folder>/` (or `$RUSTCRAFT_SAVES`): `level.ron` plus `region/r.<x>.<z>.rcr`. — `saves/` is already git-ignored; a `.app` (M13) will need a folder under `~/Library/Application Support` instead.
- 2026-10-17 — Region files hold 32×32 chunks like 1.0.0's McRegion, but in our own simpler format: a magic/version header, an (offset, length) table, then each chunk as bincode (fixed-size integers) compressed with LZ4. A save rewrites the whole region file through a temporary file and a rename instead of 1.0.0's 4 KiB sectors. — CLAUDE.md §2 names bincode + lz4_flex; whole-file rewrites can't leave a half-written file, and only changed chunks are saved, so files stay small.
- 2026-10-17 — Only chunks the player changed are saved; everything else is regenerated from the seed. A changed chunk stays in memory after unloading until the next save, then is read back from its region file. A chunk that fails to load is logged and regenerated. — CLAUDE.md M6 "only modified chunks saved".
- 2026-10-17 — The level is a readable `level.ron` (version, name, seed, game mode, spawn, player feet position and look, tick count, last played) instead of 1.0.0's NBT `level.dat`. Velocity isn't saved. Inventory (M7) and health, hunger and other stats (M9) don't exist yet; they'll be added to it then with defaults, so older saves still load. — Saving fields that don't exist would just be placeholders.
- 2026-10-17 — Autosave every 5 minutes (6000 ticks, `AUTOSAVE_TICKS`) and when the game exits by closing the window or Cmd+Q. Killing it from the terminal (Ctrl+C) doesn't save. — CLAUDE.md asks for "every few minutes"; 1.0.0 instead saved a few changed chunks every 40 ticks.
- 2026-10-17 — The main menu follows 1.0.0's screens: title (Singleplayer, Quit Game), Select World (name, folder, when last played, game mode; Play Selected World, Create New World, Cancel) and Create New World (name with the folder it will use, seed, a Game Mode button cycling Survival/Creative with 1.0.0's descriptions). Seeds and folder names follow 1.0.0: numbers are used as is, other text is hashed like Java's `String.hashCode`, blank or 0 is random; characters not allowed in file names become `_` and `-` is added until the folder is new. Rename, Delete and Hardcore are left out. — CLAUDE.md M6 asks only for create, load and quit; Hardcore is out of scope.
- 2026-10-17 — There's no way back to the title screen from a world yet: quit (which saves) and relaunch. — "Save and Quit to Title" lives in the pause menu, which is M11.
- 2026-10-17 — The game mode is saved, fixed per world and shown in F3, but behaves the same in both modes until creative mode arrives in M7. `GameMode` lives in `player/mode.rs` so every later mode check goes through it. — CLAUDE.md M7.
- 2026-10-17 — Command line: no arguments opens the main menu; `--world <name>` opens that saved world directly, creating it with `--seed <n>` and `--creative` if it doesn't exist. `--seed <n>` alone, `--flat` and `--gallery` are unsaved test worlds as before. — Lets a world be opened without clicking through the menu, for testing.
