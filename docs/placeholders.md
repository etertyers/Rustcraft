# Placeholder blocks and items

Each one has its English name, texture or model and stack size from vanilla, and does nothing beyond being placed, broken and dropped (blocks) or held (items). Later versions give them real behavior; when one gets some, move its entry from `assets/data/placeholder_*.ron` into `blocks.ron` or `items.ron`.
