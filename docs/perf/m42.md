## Profiling (384-tall world, all dimensions, bundled mods, 8-bot LAN)

`sample` on the running perf tests: the overworld's main thread is idle most of the time (waiting on the workers); no game system (random ticks, mods, farming, bobbers) shows. The workers' time is lighting and meshing: in the Nether, `light_chunk` and its flood fill were the top cost (the Nether sits in sections 4–11 of a 24-section world, so the column scan walked the empty depths and the empty sections above). Fixes: the scan skips sections below the lowest block and handles every all-air section by writing its light straight down, with no per-block lookups. The Nether's average light-and-mesh job went from 10.13 ms to 9.18 ms (M30, before the deeper world: 6.85 ms); the rest is the flood fill from lava and glowstone, which is the same work as before in a taller array. All three dimensions are far above the gate.

## The 1.2.0 app

`scripts/bundle-macos.sh` builds `Rustcraft.app` 1.2.0 (it now also ships `mods/`). Checked by hand on this Mac: it launches by `open`, opens a copy of the v1.1 world "m17 test" (the world upgrades to the 384-tall depth), loads the bundled Fullbright, and hosts a LAN world that 8 bots join (`--lan-test 8` from the app's binary).

**Gate:** pass (60+ FPS in each dimension, server ticks under 50 ms with 8 bots).
