| 100 ticks of spreading water | 3.48 ms | +1% |
| 100 ticks of redstone lines | 2.89 ms | +0% |

**Gate:** FPS passes (137 average, 64 for the 1% low). The benchmark rule (at most 15% slower than the last milestone) **fails** for chunk generation, lighting and meshing. The extra work is the 384-block world and its caves that M34 asks for, and it runs on worker threads. Tried: skipping light emitters boxed in by rock and lava (−1%), and a cached model lookup (slower, reverted). Making generated water and lava stand still cut remeshes during the flight from 6,298 to 3,693. Needs Kenzo's call: accept the new baseline, or spend a milestone on optimizing (e.g. skipping fully enclosed sections when lighting and meshing).
